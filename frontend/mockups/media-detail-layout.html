Improvements

+ Transcript visible alongside video — no tab switching needed. Auto-scrolls with playback and highlights active words.
+ All 5 analysis steps visible sequentially. Easy to scan the full pipeline without nested navigation.
+ Color-coded speaker labels make multi-participant transcripts scannable. Collapsible panel preserves full-width video when desired.

Responsive Behavior

  • On mobile / narrow screens (< 768px), the transcript panel stacks below the video player instead of sitting alongside it.
  • Analysis steps remain sequential at all breakpoints — they naturally reflow as a single column.
  • Transcript collapse toggle becomes especially useful on tablets where horizontal space is limited.
